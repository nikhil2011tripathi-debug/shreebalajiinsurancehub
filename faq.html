Frequently Asked Questions

Motor insurance

What is the difference between third-party and comprehensive insurance?

Third-party cover addresses eligible legal liability to third parties as defined by the policy and law. Comprehensive packages may also cover insured-vehicle damage subject to selected cover, exclusions and deductibles. Check the actual policy wording.

What documents are usually needed for vehicle insurance?

Vehicle registration details, existing policy information (for renewal), vehicle and owner details may be requested. The insurer may require additional documents depending on the case.

Life and health insurance

Are premiums and eligibility fixed?

No. Premiums and eligibility depend on the insurer, product, age, coverage, underwriting and other disclosed details.

What should I check in a health policy?

Review waiting periods, exclusions, room limits, co-payments, sub-limits, network information and claim procedures in the policy documents.

Commercial and property insurance

Can commercial vehicles, shops or homes be insured?

Suitable products may be available subject to insurer underwriting, property/vehicle details and policy conditions. Request a quote for the specific risk and verify inclusions and exclusions.

Finance and loans

Can you guarantee loan approval?

No. The lender decides eligibility, rates, amount and approval after its assessment.

Train and flight booking

Is a booking confirmed when I send an enquiry?

No. Booking is confirmed only when the applicable provider issues its confirmation, subject to availability, fare and rules.
